Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

You play as the rabbit on a grid, facing balloon-animal enemies. Each turn you have 3 mana and a hand of cards, and every card has a mana cost.

  • Tap a card to select it. The tiles where it can be used light up on the grid.
  • Tap a highlighted tile to play the card there. Tap outside the grid to cancel and pick another card.
  • When you're done, tap the green check-mark button to end your turn. The enemies then take their turn and your mana is refilled.
  • Defeat every enemy to win the battle. If your health reaches zero, the run is over.
What do the different cards do?
  • Attack cards damage enemies standing on the highlighted tiles around you.
  • Movement cards move the rabbit to a highlighted tile.
  • Defend cards give you a shield that absorbs damage.
  • Effect cards have special effects described on the card.

The pattern of tiles a card can reach is part of its design, so where you stand matters as much as what you play.

How many cards do I have?

You start each battle with 4 cards in hand. Every time you play a card, you draw a new one. If none of the cards in your hand can be played with your remaining mana, your turn passes automatically.

How does a run work?

A run is a series of battles. The health you have left at the end of one battle carries over into the next one, so protect it. Win every battle to beat the run. If you are defeated, you can start the run again from the beginning.

How do I pause, resume or restart?

Tap the pause button during a battle. From the pause menu you can Resume your battle or Reset the run to start over from the first battle.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Trick or Sprint works fully offline.

Is my progress saved if I close the app?

Your progress is kept only while the app is open. If you close the app in the middle of a run, you will start a new run the next time you open it.

The game is slow, frozen or crashing. What can I do?
  • Close the app completely (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again.
  • Restart your device and make sure no other heavy apps are running in the background.
  • Check that you have the latest version of Trick or Sprint and the latest iOS update available for your device.
  • Make sure your device has some free storage space.

If it keeps happening, send us the details described above and we'll look into it.

I don't feel the vibrations

The game uses your device's haptic feedback. Check that System Haptics is turned on in Settings → Sounds & Haptics, and note that some devices, including some iPads, don't have a haptic engine.

Does the game collect my data?

No. Trick or Sprint has no accounts, ads or analytics. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

Trick or Sprint runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later.
