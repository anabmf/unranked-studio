Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Trick or Sprint (the "app"), a positional card game for iPhone and iPad published by Unranked Studio ("we", "us"). The short version: we do not collect any data about you.

1. Information We Collect

Trick or Sprint is a self-contained, offline game. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account needed. You never have to sign up, log in, or give us a name or email address to play.
  • No personal data. We do not collect names, contact details, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or any other information about you.
  • No analytics or advertising. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking or attribution tools.
  • No purchases. The app has no in-app purchases, so no payment information is ever handled by us.
  • No device permissions. The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or any other private data on your device.

2. How the App Works on Your Device

All gameplay, including your cards, your position on the grid, your health and the enemies' moves, is calculated locally on your device. The app does not need an internet connection to run and does not send gameplay information to us or to anyone else.

The app uses your device's haptic engine to give small vibrations when you play cards or tap buttons. This happens entirely on your device and no data is recorded or transmitted.

3. Third-Party Services

Trick or Sprint is built with the Unity game engine. We have not enabled Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity In-App Purchasing or any other third-party service that collects data about players, and the app includes no third-party advertising or social SDKs.

If you have chosen to share iOS analytics with app developers in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect. These reports cannot be used to identify you, and sharing them is controlled by you in your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children. Parents and guardians can be confident that the app contains no ads, no external links to outside services, no chat and no user-generated content.

5. Your Rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you contact us by email (see below), we will only use your address to reply to you, and we will delete the conversation on request.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we add new features that change how data is handled (for example, online features), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the new version of the app is released.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or about how Trick or Sprint works, please reach out to Unranked Studio:
