Trick or Sprint

Every great magician starts as a rabbit.

A positional card game where where you stand matters as much as what you play. Hop across the grid, line up your cards and outsmart a circus of balloon animals.

The Game

Trick or Sprint is a turn-based, positional card game for iPhone and iPad. Every card is a pattern on the grid, so the best move is rarely the strongest card, it's the one you can actually reach.

1

Position is power

Cards hit or move you across specific tiles around the rabbit. Step into the right spot first, then strike.

2

Spend your mana

Three mana each turn and a hand of four cards. Choose between attacking, moving and shielding.

3

Fight the balloons

Balloon dogs, turtles and fish want to pop your act. Dodge, outflank and pop them first.

4

Survive the run

Your health carries over from battle to battle. Make every hop count.

The Story

For years, a rabbit lived inside a magician's top hat, pulled out for the applause and tucked away before anyone could ask who they really were.

One night, the rabbit ran. They hopped out of the hat, grabbed a tiny hat and wand of their own, and set off to prove they're more than someone else's trick.

Now every step is a sprint, every card is a spell, and the next act is theirs.
